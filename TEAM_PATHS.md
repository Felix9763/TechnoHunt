### Mechanical Flow Overview

1. **Check-in Envelope (Clue 1):** Physical envelope handed to team. Contains starting riddle pointing to their **Clue 2 Zone**.
2. **Clue 2 (Physical Codeword at Zone):** Team walks to Zone. Searches for the physical clue card taped on site. Solves the puzzle and submits the codeword on the website.
3. **Crewmate (Physical Volunteer Actor):** Website displays witness photo + statement. **No location is shown!** Team must spot this volunteer roaming campus on foot. Volunteer speaks in character and hands over a physical slip containing the **Scrambled Code**.
4. **Clue 3 (Online Decode Screen):** Team enters the slip code online. The website presents a digital decrypt screen (Hex / Binary / Keypad / ASCII / Caesar). Team solves the cipher online to unlock their **Clue 4 Destination Sector**.
5. **Clue 4 (Physical Evidence at Zone):** Team runs to the sector. Searches for the physical evidence question hidden on site. Calculates the solution and enters it on the website.
6. **Finale (Empty Stage):** System unlocks Empty Stage. 3 physical keys are hidden backstage. First 3 teams to find a key and present it to organizers win 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place.
